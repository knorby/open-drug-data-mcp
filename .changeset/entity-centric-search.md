Return evidence-grouped entities from product and ingredient searches instead
of source groups, with attributed records, confirmed links, conflicts,
candidates, per-record lookup actions, source statuses, and coverage. Bound
search enrichment to five entities, concurrency three, within a configurable
45-second shared deadline. Keep completed results on timeout and preserve
retained lookup ids when bounding responses. Align research guidance with
name-first search, category routing, CID resolution, and selective source+id
lookups and URL reading.
